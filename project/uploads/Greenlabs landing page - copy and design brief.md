# Greenlabs landing page — copy and design brief

Oct 6, 2026 · @Jean-Christophe Cuveler

## Page content

Every line below is final and goes on the page as written, in this order. The on-page section labels are the headings; the brief tells the designer not to add, cut or reword anything.

### Hero

Greenlabs

Proof, not promises.

An evening, an afternoon or a weekend — and something that works before we leave.

### How it goes

Greenlabs comes in three sizes.

S — Night. An evening, monthly, free. One thing shown working, one thing you try with your own hands, a drink.

M — Workshop. An afternoon. Build one object and take it home. Materials at cost.

L — Weekend. Once a year. The problems that don't fit in an afternoon.

Everything built is shown at the next Night and published for anyone to build.

### Who this is for

You don't need to be an engineer, an activist or a dreamer. You need an evening.

We need makers, coders, designers, people who know how a commune works, people who know the neighbourhood — and cooks. Every weekend needs cooks.

### The rules

1. It has to work before we leave.
2. Everything is open: the plans, the code, the mistakes.
3. The smallest version must be buildable at home.
4. If it makes life worse, it doesn't count. Consuming less is a design problem, not a sacrifice.
5. We build in places that already exist — fablabs, tiers-lieux, workshops. We don't build a place.

### Three ways in

Come. Host. Build.

Come to a Night. It's free.

Host one, if you run a fablab, a tiers-lieu or a workshop.

Help build the first edition.

There's no sign-up yet. When there's a date, it will be here.

### In the workshop right now

An open-source app for organizations with local, regional and national layers, so members find information and events at every level without handing their data to a platform. The code goes public at the first Night.

First Night: Brussels, winter 2026–27.

### Footer

Greenlabs is a program, not a place, and not yet an association. It runs in other people's workshops and handles no money.

Ni rêveurs, ni prêcheurs : des bricoleurs.

## Design brief for Claude Design

Design a single static landing page for Greenlabs, typographic and paper-like, using the copy above verbatim. Nothing on it is clickable: no form, no buttons, no navigation, no links. Its only action is reading.

### What Greenlabs is

Greenlabs is a program, not a place and not yet an association. It runs maker events in three sizes — an evening (Night), an afternoon (Workshop), a weekend (Weekend) — where people build working answers to ecological problems, inside fablabs and tiers-lieux that already exist. First edition: Brussels, winter 2026–27. The attitude is "proof, not promises": everything should feel made rather than marketed.

### Audience

Curious people who might come to one evening — not engineers, activists or "eco" people in particular. Secondarily, people who run a fablab, a tiers-lieu or a workshop and might host. Brussels first, international later, so the page must read naturally to non-native English readers.

### Tone

A lab notebook, not a brochure. Confident, dry, a little witty, zero eco-clichés. Plain words, lots of air.

### Copy rules

- Use the page content verbatim, in that order, with its punctuation, dashes and the French sign-off line.
- Do not add headlines, taglines, subtitles, testimonials, statistics, badges, calls to action, "learn more" lines or sections.
- Do not shorten the five rules or merge the two sentences of rule 4.
- Keep sentence case everywhere; only the mono section labels are uppercase.
- The only text not in the content section is the title tag and meta description.

### Visual direction

- Typographic page. No photographs, no stock imagery, no illustrations.
- No nature iconography: no leaves, globes, seedlings, hands, recycling arrows, wind turbines, trees.
- No icons at all except the size motif below.
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no rounded "SaaS" cards, no hero illustration, no partner logos, no social icons.

### Colour

- Paper (light background): #F3EFE6.
- Ink (text, outlines): #161616.
- Rules and dividers: ink at 20% opacity.
- Accent: #B8FF3C, an acid green, read as a status LED meaning "it works".
- Accent use is sparing: the LED dot before the tagline, the dot in the corner of the Night square, rule-number underlines. Never a background fill, never a large area.
- On the light paper the accent is never used for text (contrast is too low); size letters and rule numbers are ink with an accent underline.
- Dark mode: paper #141414, ink #F3EFE6, same accent; there the accent may be used for the size letters and rule numbers as text.

### Typography

- IBM Plex Sans for the wordmark, headings and body; IBM Plex Mono for section labels, size letters, rule numbers, status lines and the footer. Both via Google Fonts (Sans 400, 500, 600; Mono 400, 500). IBM Plex is open source, which suits an open-source program.
- Wordmark "Greenlabs": Plex Sans 500, 40px mobile / 56px desktop, ink, no logo mark.
- Tagline "Proof, not promises.": Plex Sans 500, 28px / 36px, preceded by a 10px accent LED dot on the baseline.
- Lead: Plex Sans 400, 20px / 22px.
- Body: Plex Sans 400, 18px, line height 1.55, measure 60–70 characters.
- Section labels: Plex Mono 500, 13px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em, ink at 60%.
- Size letters S, M, L: Plex Mono 500, 28px.

### Motif

Three squares of increasing size, side by side, outlined in ink at 1.5px, proportions 1 : 1.6 : 2.6, with S, M and L in mono beneath. The S square carries a small filled accent dot in its lower-right corner (the LED). It appears twice: large in "How it goes" as the mark on each of the three cards, and tiny (16px tall) in the footer beside the status line. Nowhere else.

### Layout

- One column, left-aligned, maximum text width 640px, centred on desktop with generous margins. Mobile first: 16px side gutters at 390px, no horizontal scroll, readable at 380px.
- Vertical rhythm: 64px between sections on mobile, 96px on desktop. Sections separated by a 1px rule at 20% ink, like ruled notebook lines.
- Each section opens with its mono label, uppercase: HOW IT GOES, WHO THIS IS FOR, THE RULES, THREE WAYS IN, IN THE WORKSHOP RIGHT NOW. The hero and the footer carry no label.
- Hero: wordmark, then LED dot + tagline, then the lead. Nothing else above the fold on mobile.
- How it goes: the intro line, then three cards — stacked on mobile, three equal columns from 900px. Each card: its square from the motif, the size letter, the name (Night / Workshop / Weekend) in Plex Sans 500, the descriptor in body size. Cards have no border and no fill; a top rule separates them on mobile.
- The rules: a numbered list 1–5, numbers in mono with the accent underline, each rule on its own line, 24px between rules.
- Three ways in: "Come. Host. Build." in Plex Sans 500 at lead size, then the three lines as body, then the no-sign-up line in mono 14px at 60% ink.
- In the workshop right now: the paragraph in body, then "First Night: Brussels, winter 2026–27." set as a mono 16px status line with the accent LED dot before it.
- Footer: the tiny motif, the status sentence in mono 14px at 60% ink, then the French line in Plex Sans 400 italic at body size.

### Dark mode

Follow the system preference. Same layout, same type; only the paper, ink and accent-as-text rules above change.

### Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast for all text in both modes.
- Semantic structure: one h1 (the wordmark), h2 for each section label, an ordered list for the rules, a footer element.
- Text remains readable with fonts blocked (system sans and mono fallbacks).

### Motion

None, with one allowed exception: the LED dot before the tagline may pulse once, slowly, in the first two seconds after load. Nothing else moves, no scroll effects.

### Deliverables

1. The page at 1440px and at 390px, in light and dark mode.
2. The build-ready version as a single self-contained page, fonts loaded from Google Fonts only.

## Decisions baked in

Eight choices removed every placeholder; three of them commit Greenlabs to something and are marked as such.

1. Formats are named Night, Workshop and Weekend, with S, M and L as their size labels. The names are what people say; the sizes are the visual device and work in any language.
2. Version 1 is in English, with the French sign-off line as the only other-language text. French and Dutch versions follow once the first Night has a host.
3. No contact, no form, no newsletter, no social links. The page says so in one line instead of hiding it.
4. The first Night is announced as Brussels, winter 2026–27. This binds: it is the one line that makes the page a plan rather than a brochure, and the season is the commitment, not a date.
5. The code of the app goes public at the first Night. This binds: it ties the only promise on the page to the only date on the page.
6. The app is described, not named, and no partner or first adopter is named. A name set on this page becomes the name by default; naming waits for the second adopter.
7. Nights are free, Workshop materials are at cost paid to the host, Greenlabs handles no money. This binds: it is a rule on the page and a rule for how the first editions run.
8. The page is typographic, in IBM Plex, paper and ink with one acid-green accent, and carries no photograph. A photo of a real object built at home can replace the hero lead once one exists.
