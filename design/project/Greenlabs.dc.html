An evening, an afternoon or a weekend — and something that works before we leave.

How it goes

Greenlabs comes in three sizes.

S

Night

An evening, monthly, free. One thing shown working, one thing you try with your own hands, a drink.

M

Workshop

An afternoon. Build one object and take it home. Materials at cost.

L

Weekend

Once a year. The problems that don't fit in an afternoon.

Everything built is shown at the next Night and published for anyone to build.

Who this is for

You don't need to be an engineer, an activist or a dreamer. You need an evening.

We need makers, coders, designers, people who know how a commune works, people who know the neighbourhood — and cooks. Every weekend needs cooks.

The rules

  1. 1 It has to work before we leave.
  2. 2 Everything is open: the plans, the code, the mistakes.
  3. 3 The smallest version must be buildable at home.
  4. 4 If it makes life worse, it doesn't count. Consuming less is a design problem, not a sacrifice.
  5. 5 We build in places that already exist — fablabs, tiers-lieux, workshops. We don't build a place.
